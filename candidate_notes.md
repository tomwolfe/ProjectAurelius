## Candidate Rationale Notes - Battery Electrolyte Candidates

1. **FCCOC(=O)OC** (Fluoroethylene carbonate, FEC): FEC promotes stable SEI formation on silicon anodes and enhances cycling stability in Na-ion systems through fluorinated decomposition products.

2. **CS(=O)(=O)C** (Dimethyl sulfone, MS): Dimethyl sulfone provides high-voltage stability (>4.5V vs Na/Na+) and forms robust inorganic-rich SEI layers on anode surfaces.

3. **N#CCCC#N** (Succinonitrile, SN): Succinonitrile enables high ionic conductivity in solid polymer electrolytes and forms beneficial NaF-rich SEI on anode interfaces.

4. **COC(=O)OC(F)(F)F** (Methyl trifluoromethyl carbonate): Trifluoromethyl substitution enhances oxidative stability and promotes formation of thin, ionically conductive SEI layers.

5. **CCOC(=O)OC(C)(C)C** (Ethyl tert-butyl carbonate): Bulky tert-butyl groups reduce reactivity at high voltage while maintaining low viscosity for fast ion transport.

6. **COC(=O)OCC(F)(F)F** (Trifluoroethyl methyl carbonate): Dual fluorination on the ethyl chain provides exceptional high-voltage stability (>4.8V) and low nucleophilicity at the anode interface.

7. **C1COC(=O)O1** (Gamma-butyrolactone, GBL): Cyclic ester structure offers high dielectric constant for salt dissociation and forms stable SEI when co-solvated with fluorinated additives.

8. **CC(C)OC(=O)OC(C)C** (Diisopropyl carbonate): Branched alkyl structure suppresses dendrite growth through steric hindrance while maintaining adequate ionic conductivity.
